4. HERO INTERACTION
==================================================

Build a visually striking but lightweight hero.

Required layers:
1. responsive background image through <picture>
2. dark navy readability gradients
3. a very slow Ken Burns scale animation from 1 to 1.04
4. a custom Canvas component called GoldenDustCanvas
5. subtle pointer parallax capped at 8px

GoldenDustCanvas requirements:
- 28–40 particles on mobile, 60–85 on desktop
- low-opacity gold tones
- particle radius 0.5–2px
- very slow movement
- capped devicePixelRatio of 2
- ResizeObserver
- pause when the page is hidden
- clean up requestAnimationFrame and listeners on unmount
- pointer-events: none
- aria-hidden=true
- the effect must never block scrolling or clicks

Respect prefers-reduced-motion: disable Ken Burns, particles, parallax and reveal movement when reduced motion is enabled.

Do not use an autoplay video in version 1. The dynamic image/canvas solution should provide the visual impact with much better mobile performance.
